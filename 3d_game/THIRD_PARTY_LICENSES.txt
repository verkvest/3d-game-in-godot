Pyrewick
First-person vault game built on the Brackeys "3D game in Godot" project.
Every bundled asset below may be redistributed commercially.

This file is shown in the in-game Credits & licenses screen.
No third-party fonts are bundled (Godot's built-in font is used).
No music is bundled.
